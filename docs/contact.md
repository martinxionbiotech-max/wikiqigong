# Contact & Corrections

**WikiQigong is edited in public.** The most reliable way to reach the people
responsible for a page is the public repository issue tracker. This keeps the
correction traceable and gives other readers the chance to see the source that
was used.

## Where to write

- **Corrections and editorial questions:**
  <https://github.com/martinxionbiotech-max/wikiqigong/issues>
- **Privacy requests:** use the same issue tracker, but do not post sensitive
  personal information. Say that you need a private channel and we will arrange
  one.
- **Repository:** <https://github.com/martinxionbiotech-max/wikiqigong>

There is no WikiQigong account system, checkout, or telephone support line.
The site is an educational publication, not a clinic or a commercial store.

## What to include in a correction

A useful correction is specific enough that we can check it without guessing:

1. **The page URL** — for example, `/articles/what-is-qigong/`.
2. **The exact sentence** — copy the words that are wrong, not just the topic.
3. **The problem** — a wrong character, tone mark, date, name, citation, or a
   health claim that overstates the evidence.
4. **The source** — a book, paper, institution page, or other checkable
   reference. If you are reporting a typo you do not need a source.
5. **Your preferred correction**, if you have one.

We will review the source, correct the page if the evidence supports it, and
mark the change in the repository. We may disagree when the source is ambiguous;
in that case we will explain the wording rather than silently changing it.

## What not to send

Please do not send:

- medical records, symptoms, diagnoses, or treatment questions;
- government identifiers, payment card data, or bank details;
- confidential manuscripts or material you do not have permission to share;
- personal information about another person.

WikiQigong does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or individual
practice prescriptions. A question about a health condition belongs with a
qualified healthcare professional, not with this editorial team.

## Response expectations

This is a small editorial project. We aim to acknowledge a clear correction
within a few business days and to review it in the next editorial pass. Complex
source disputes can take longer. The public issue remains the record; please do
not open duplicate issues for the same point.

## Advertising and commercial enquiries

WikiQigong displays advertising through Google AdSense. Advertisers do not
choose or approve editorial content, and the presence of an ad is not an
endorsement of the advertised product or service. There is no paid placement
inside articles. For a privacy question about advertising data, see the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## Before you write

If your question is about a practice or a health condition, read the
[Medical Disclaimer](/disclaimer/) first. If your question is about how pages
are written and reviewed, see [Editorial Standards](/editorial-standards/) and
the [Editorial Team](/team/).

Thank you for helping keep the Chinese terms, the history, and the evidence
accurate.
