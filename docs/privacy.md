# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 19 November 2026**

WikiQigong is an independent educational publication about tai chi, qigong and
Chinese mind–body practice. This policy explains what information is processed
when you read the site, why it is processed, and what choices you have. It
applies to `wikiqigong.com` and its subpages.

We have tried to keep this policy readable. If a sentence is unclear, please
ask us through the public repository contact channel listed at the end; we will
clarify the policy rather than ask you to accept an unexplained term.

## 1. Who is responsible

The site is published by the **WikiQigong editorial team**. The team is
responsible for the editorial content and for the limited data processing
described here. WikiQigong is an educational publication; it is not a clinic,
a medical practice, or a seller of health services.

Because this is a small independent publication, the practical contact route is
the public repository:

- **Repository:** <https://github.com/martinxionbiotech-max/wikiqigong>
- **Contact method:** open an issue in that repository and describe the privacy
  question without including sensitive personal information.

If a matter requires a private channel, say so in the issue without posting the
sensitive details; we will arrange a suitable route. Please do not send medical
records, government identifiers, payment details, or other sensitive material
through a public issue.

## 2. What we process

### 2.1 Information you choose to send

The site has no account registration, no checkout, no comment system and no
contact form. If you open a GitHub issue or send a correction, GitHub processes
the information you submit under its own terms. We receive the content of that
issue, your GitHub username and whatever information you choose to include. We
use it to answer the question, correct the page, or maintain an editorial
record.

Please include only what is needed for the correction: the page URL, the exact
sentence, and the source that should be checked. Do not include personal
medical information.

### 2.2 Technical information from hosting

Like most static websites, the hosting and content-delivery providers process
technical request data in order to serve the site and protect it from abuse.
That can include an IP address, browser user agent, requested URL, referrer,
timestamp, and response status. The exact fields depend on the hosting provider
and its retention settings. We do not use this information to identify
individual readers, and we do not combine it with any other data to build a
profile.

### 2.3 Search

The site's search function runs against a static search index served with the
site. It does not require an account and does not send your query to a
WikiQigong account system. Your browser may keep local state for the interface,
but that state is not a tracking profile.

### 2.4 Advertising and Google AdSense

The site displays advertising supplied by **Google AdSense**. When advertising
is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage, web beacons
or similar technologies to serve ads, measure their performance, and limit how
often you see the same ad.

Google may use information about your visits to this and other websites to
provide ads that are more relevant to you. Depending on your region and your
consent choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Google's own
description of how it uses information from sites that use its services is at
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

You can learn about and control personalised advertising through:

- **Google Ads Settings:** <https://adssettings.google.com/>
- **Your Online Choices (EU/UK):** <https://www.youronlinechoices.com/>
- **About Ads (US):** <https://optout.aboutads.info/>

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland,
consent for advertising cookies is requested through a Google-certified consent
management platform where required. You can change or withdraw consent through
the privacy message shown on the site or through the controls Google provides.
Refusing consent does not prevent you from reading the editorial pages; it may
mean that ads are non-personalised or not shown.

We do not sell personal information. We do not operate a first-party advertising
profile, and we do not receive the identity of individual readers from Google.

## 3. Why we process information

We process the limited information described above for these purposes:

1. **Deliver the website** — serve the pages, assets and search index you
   request.
2. **Keep the site secure** — detect abusive requests, spam and attempts to
   disrupt the service.
3. **Respond to corrections** — understand and act on a reported factual error.
4. **Display advertising** — allow Google AdSense to serve and measure ads,
   subject to your consent choices where required.
5. **Comply with legal obligations** — retain or disclose information when the
   law requires it, after checking the request.

We do not use the site to make automated decisions about you, and we do not
use reader data to train a WikiQigong model.

## 4. Legal bases (EEA/UK readers)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following bases:

- **Legitimate interests** for serving a static website, maintaining security,
  and responding to corrections. We keep this processing minimal and do not
  build reader profiles.
- **Consent** for advertising cookies and similar technologies where consent is
  required. You can withdraw consent at any time through the consent controls.
- **Legal obligation** where we must retain or disclose a record to comply with
  applicable law.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

WikiQigong itself does not set first-party analytics or advertising cookies.
The site is generated as static HTML and does not include a WikiQigong account
cookie.

Advertising supplied by Google AdSense may set or read cookies and local
storage on your device. Those technologies are controlled by Google and its
partners, not by WikiQigong. The categories can include:

- **Advertising delivery** — selecting an ad, limiting repetition, and
  measuring whether an ad was shown.
- **Fraud prevention** — detecting invalid traffic and protecting advertisers.
- **Consent state** — remembering the choice you made in the consent message.

Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies. Blocking advertising
cookies does not block the editorial content. If you use a content blocker, the
site should continue to work; some ads may not appear.

## 6. Sharing

We share information only in the limited ways needed to run the site:

- **Google AdSense and its partners** process advertising-related data as
  described above.
- **Hosting and CDN providers** process request data to deliver and protect the
  site.
- **GitHub** processes repository issues and related account data under its own
  terms.
- **Authorities** where disclosure is required by law, after a request has been
  reviewed.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not share reader
data with advertisers as a named list or contact database.

## 7. International transfers

The site is available worldwide, and the service providers above may process
data in countries other than yours. Where required, transfers rely on the
provider's safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses. Because the site
is a static educational publication, the data involved is limited to request
logs, public issue content, and advertising data controlled by Google.

## 8. Retention

- **Hosting logs** are retained for the period set by the hosting provider for
  security and operations, then deleted or aggregated.
- **Public issues** remain available for as long as the repository is public,
  unless GitHub or the repository maintainers remove them. Do not post
  sensitive information in an issue.
- **Advertising data** is retained by Google according to Google's policies and
  your account settings.

We do not keep a separate WikiQigong reader database, because there is no
account system.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- ask what personal information is held about you;
- ask for a correction;
- ask for deletion;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- withdraw advertising consent;
- lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

To make a request, use the repository contact route and describe the request
without posting sensitive details. We may need to verify that a request relates
to you before acting on it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising
a privacy right.

## 10. Children

WikiQigong is an educational site intended for a general audience. It is not
directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal
information through a public issue, contact us so the content can be reviewed
and removed where appropriate.

## 11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We keep the editorial repository public and
review changes before publication. No internet transmission is completely
secure; please do not send sensitive personal, medical or financial information
through a public issue or any other public channel.

## 12. External links

Articles link to research papers, institutions and other educational resources.
Those sites have their own privacy policies. Following an external link takes
you outside WikiQigong, and this policy does not govern the destination site.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site's data practices change, for example
when advertising, consent management or hosting changes. The effective date at
the top shows the current version. Material changes will be described on this
page. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

## 14. Contact

For a privacy question, correction, or request, use the repository issue
tracker:

<https://github.com/martinxionbiotech-max/wikiqigong/issues>

Please include the page URL and the nature of the request, but no sensitive
personal data. For a correction to an article, the most useful message includes
the exact sentence and the source that should be checked.
