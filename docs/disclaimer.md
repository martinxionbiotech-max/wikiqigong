# Medical Disclaimer

**WikiQigong is an educational resource. It is not medical advice.**

The content on this site is provided for general educational and informational
purposes only. It describes traditional Chinese mind–body practices — tai chi,
qigong, meditation, and breathing — and their historical and cultural context.

## What we are not

- We are **not** providing medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
- We are **not** recommending any practice as a substitute for professional
  medical care.
- We are **not** claiming that any practice described here cures, treats,
  prevents, or reverses any disease or health condition.

## About our health wording

Where research is discussed, we describe findings cautiously and with their
actual strength of evidence. Terms like *"may support,"* *"may help,"* and
*"has been studied for"* are used deliberately. They are not marketing
language; they reflect the current, often preliminary state of the evidence
base for mind–body practices.

## Before you practice

- If you have a medical condition — especially cardiovascular, respiratory,
  balance-related, or musculoskeletal — consult a qualified healthcare
  professional before beginning any new movement or breathing practice.
- If you are pregnant, consult your care provider before beginning.
- Stop any practice that causes pain, dizziness, breathlessness, or distress.

## Seeking help

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency
services immediately.
