   - Closes the middle layer of xing-yi-qi. Etymology: 心 ("heart-mind")
     beneath 音 ("sound") — the mind attending to a sound, i.e. attention

   - Closes the tailbone-gate gap from san-guan / xiao-zhou-tian.

     spine. Modern note: "drop the tailbone" = neutral pelvis + long spine.
